Omborchi — HED Electronix omborida tovar qoldig'ini sanash uchun ichki veb-ilova. Ilova faqat egasi ruxsat bergan Google akauntlar uchun ishlaydi.

Qanday ma'lumot olinadi

Google bilan kirishda ilova faqat sizning email manzilingizni tekshiradi (ruxsat ro'yxatida borligini bilish uchun). Ism, rasm yoki boshqa Google ma'lumotlariga kirilmaydi va saqlanmaydi.

Ombor ma'lumotlari

Mahsulotlar, qoldiqlar va harakatlar tarixi ilova egasining Google Drive'idagi faylda saqlanadi va faqat ruxsat berilgan foydalanuvchilarga ko'rinadi. Ma'lumotlar uchinchi shaxslarga sotilmaydi va reklama uchun ishlatilmaydi.

Omborchi — Privacy Policy

Omborchi is an internal web app used by HED Electronix to count warehouse stock. It is available only to Google accounts approved by the owner.

Data we use. When you sign in with Google, the app checks only your email address to confirm you are on the allow-list. It does not access or store your name, photo or any other Google data.

Warehouse data. Products, stock levels and history are stored in a file in the owner's Google Drive and are visible only to approved users. Data is never sold or used for advertising.
